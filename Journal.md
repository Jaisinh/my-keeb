# Day 1 
I spent quite a bit of time looking at different keyboards and layouts to find something that matched the vibe I was going for. After exploring a bunch of options, I found myself really liking the layout of the Work Louder K.NOB keyboard.

![K.NOB Keyboard](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/440b7704-16d5-4e64-beb6-d1b7dc961513)

I don't plan to copy it exactly though. My current idea is to keep the overall layout but use MX-style keycaps instead of the stock ones. With the layout mostly decided, my next step is to look into switches and figure out what typing feel I want.

I also got KiCad installed today, so I'm finally ready to start working on the actual PCB side of things.
